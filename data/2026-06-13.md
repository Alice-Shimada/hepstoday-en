### [1] [Fermion Families and Pontryagin Class: Topological Field Theory via Colour Symmetry Extension](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26202)
*Zheyan Wan,Juven Wang,Shing-Tung Yau*

Main category: hep-th

TL;DR: This paper addresses the 'family puzzle'—why the Standard Model has exactly three generations—by analyzing discrete anomalies (specifically Z_n-symmetry) in 4D fermionic theories using spin bordism and group cohomology. It shows that only certain anomalies (group-cohomology type) can be canceled by anomalous topological quantum field theories (TQFTs), while others involving Pontryagin class p₁ cannot—except for n=2,3. A key result is that a Z₃-symmetric 4D Z₃-gauge TQFT cancels the mixed (B+L)-gauge-gravitational anomaly without sterile neutrinos when n=3. Extending to generalized SM with N_c colors and N_f families, anomaly cancellation forces specific minimal integer solutions; requiring baryons to be fermions (odd N_c) singles out N_c = N_f = 3 as unique.

Motivation: To explain the origin of exactly three fermion families in the Standard Model ('family puzzle') via topological and anomaly constraints—specifically leveraging discrete Z_n symmetries, fermionic anomalies, and bordism classification in 4D/5D.

Method: Uses 5-dimensional spin bordism groups to classify 4D fermionic anomalies with discrete Z_n symmetry; distinguishes group-cohomology anomalies H⁵(Z_n,U(1)) from beyond-group-cohomology terms like A_{Z_n}p₁; constructs symmetry-extended TQFTs (e.g., via Z_n → Z_{n²} extension) to trivialize cocycles; applies anomaly inflow and symmetry extension techniques to SM-like gauge-gravity mixed anomalies, particularly (B+L)-related ones.

Result: Only H⁵(Z_n,U(1)) ≅ Z_n anomalies are cancelable by Z_n-gauge TQFTs; A_{Z_n}p₁ anomalies are not cancelable except for n=2,3. For n=3, a Spin×Z₃-symmetric 4D Z₃-gauge TQFT cancels the (B+L)-gauge-gravitational anomaly without sterile ν_R. In generalized SM with N_c colors/N_f families, anomaly cancellation implies minimal solutions: (N_c,N_f) = (3,≥3), (4,≥2), (12,≥6); imposing odd N_c (so baryons are fermions) uniquely selects (N_c,N_f)=(3,3). Also proves A_{Z₃}p₁ ≡ 0 mod 3 in appropriate cohomological context.

Conclusion: The requirement of consistent anomaly cancellation—via symmetric anomalous TQFTs built from discrete symmetry extensions—selects exactly three families and three colors as the unique minimal solution compatible with Standard Model quantum numbers and spin-statistics, offering a topological explanation for the family puzzle.

Abstract: Family puzzle asks why the Standard Model (SM) features exactly 3 families of quarks and leptons. Motivated by topological constraints, we study 4-dimensional fermionic anomalies with discrete $Z_n$ symmetry, classified by the 5d spin bordism group. We show that only the group-cohomology subclass H$^5(Z_n,U(1))\cong Z_n$ can be canceled by an anomalous $Z_n$-symmetric 4d $Z_n$-gauge topological quantum field theory (TQFT), while beyond-group-cohomology $A_{Z_n} p_1$ involving the Pontryagin class $p_1$ cannot (except $n=2,3$). More generally, we prove that any cocycle $α_d\in$H$^d(Z_n,U(1))$ in odd spacetime dimension $d\ge3$ is trivialised by the symmetry extension $1\to Z_n\to Z_{n^2}\to Z_n\to 1,$ and we construct the corresponding symmetric anomalous boundary TQFT. For $d=5$ and $n=3$, this yields a Spin$\times Z_3$-symmetric 4d $Z_3$-gauge TQFT that cancels the mixed discrete $(\bf B+L)$-gauge-gravitational anomaly of the SM in the absence of 3 "sterile" right-handed neutrinos $ν_R$. We further analyze a generalized SM with $N_c$ colors and $N_f$ families and argue that missing $N_f$ copies of the $ν_R$ can be naturally replaced by that 4d anomalous $Spin\times_{Z_2^F} Z_{2 N_f,{{\bf B} + {\bf L}}}$ symmetric $Z_{N_c}$-gauge TQFT under the anomaly cancellation, via an appropriate $Z_{N_c}$-color symmetry extension construction $1\to Z_{N_c}\to Spin\times Z_{N_cN_f}\to Spin\times_{Z_2^F} Z_{2N_f}\to1$ of anomalous topological order. For minimal nonzero positive integers $N_c$ and $N_f$, we find the minimal color extensions: $N_c=3, N_f \ge 3$; $N_c=4, N_f \ge 2$; and $N_c=12, N_f \ge 6$. If we further require that an SM baryon is a fermion so $N_c$ is odd, then $Z_{N_c}=Z($SU$(N_c))$ color center, we prove 3 families and 3 colors, $N_c=N_f=3$, is the unique case that stands out. We also prove that $A_{Z_3}p_1= 0\mod3$ for the mod 3 cohomology class in an appropriate context.
