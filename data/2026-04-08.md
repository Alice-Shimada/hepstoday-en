### [1] [Measurement of charged-particle production in $\sqrt{s_\text{NN}}=9.62$ TeV proton-oxygen collisions as a probe of cosmic-ray air showers with the ATLAS detector](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05512)
*ATLAS Collaboration*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: This paper reports the first measurement of prompt charged-particle production in proton-oxygen collisions at √s_NN = 9.62 TeV using ATLAS, yielding high-precision fiducial and extrapolated cross sections and particle distributions that significantly constrain hadronic interaction models for cosmic-ray air shower simulations.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: To provide high-precision experimental data on proton–nucleus collisions to test and improve hadronic-interaction models used in cosmic-ray air shower simulations—critical for astroparticle physics.

Method: Analysis of 246 million proton–oxygen collision events recorded by the ATLAS detector at √s_NN = 9.62 TeV, with integrated luminosity 634 μb⁻¹; selection requires at least one charged track with p_T > 500 MeV and |η| < 2.5; fiducial cross section measured directly, inelastic p–air cross section extrapolated using oxygen-to-air composition scaling; systematic uncertainties include experimental, luminosity, and theoretical (model-dependent) components.

Result: Fiducial pO cross section: σ_fid^pO = 396 ± 6 (exp.) ± 9 (lumi.) mb; extrapolated inelastic p–air cross section: σ_inel^p+air = 406 ± 6 (exp.) ± 9 (lumi.) ± 28 (th.) mb; charged-particle multiplicity, p_T, and η distributions achieved ~10× better precision than model discrepancies.

Conclusion: These results provide the most precise experimental constraints to date on hadronic-interaction models in the forward/central region for proton–nucleus collisions at LHC energies, directly improving the modeling of extensive air showers induced by ultra-high-energy cosmic rays.

Abstract: This Letter presents a measurement of prompt charged-particle production in proton-oxygen interactions at $\sqrt{s_\text{NN}}=9.62$ TeV center-of-mass energy with the ATLAS detector, corresponding to 634 $μ$b$^{-1}$ of integrated luminosity. A total of 246 million selected events have at least one track with transverse momentum $p_\text{T}> 500$ MeV and pseudorapidity $|η|<2.5$. The measured fiducial proton-oxygen cross section is $σ_\text{fid.}^{p\text{O}}=396 \pm 6~(\text{exp.}) \pm 9~(\text{lumi.})~\text{mb}$ and the extrapolated inelastic proton-air cross section is $σ^{p+\text{air}}_\text{inel.} = 406 \pm 6~(\text{exp.}) \pm 9~(\text{lumi.}) \pm 28~(\text{th.})~\text{mb}$. Measurements of charged-particle multiplicity, $p_\text{T}$, and $η$ distributions are an order-of-magnitude more precise than differences between hadronic-interaction models. These results enable improved modeling of cosmic-ray air showers, which is important for astroparticle physics.

</details>
