### [1] [A High-Precision Clock Synchronization System for the CEPC Accelerator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11590)
*Jun Hu,Xin Zhou,Xiaoshan Jiang,Dapeng Jin*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: This paper presents an enhanced White Rabbit-based clock synchronization system for the CEPC, achieving sub-10 ps precision via hardware redesign (Si5345A DSPLL), GTX phase alignment, and a cascaded global-control architecture with TD3-tuned PID and temperature feed-forward compensation.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: To meet the CEPC's stringent 30 ps (std. dev.) clock synchronization requirement across 192 control nodes over a 100 km tunnel, addressing limitations of standard White Rabbit—namely analog actuation noise and restart-induced timing uncertainty.

Method: Redesigned WR slave node: replaced DAC+VCXO+multiplier PLL with Si5345A DSPLL featuring DCO-based phase control; implemented GTX transceiver phase alignment and manual byte-alignment to suppress restart jitter; introduced cascaded global-control architecture with PC-side PID auto-tuned by TD3 reinforcement learning and on-chip temperature feed-forward calibrated to −0.76 ps/°C.

Result: Point-to-point sync: 3.38 ps (1 m fiber), 3.92 ps (50 km); 12-level cascade: 6.66 ps (constant temp), 7.30 ps (13°C swing); TIE jitter <1 ps regardless of cascade depth; restart uncertainty: 2.82 ps (std. dev.); 4-level cascade stable for 25 hours.

Conclusion: The enhanced WR system fully satisfies the CEPC’s 30 ps synchronization budget, demonstrating scalability, thermal robustness, and long-term stability—enabling high-precision distributed timing for large-scale particle accelerator infrastructure.

Abstract: The Circular Electron Positron Collider (CEPC) distributes a reference clock distributed to 192 control nodes along its 100~km underground tunnel. The required synchronization precision is 30~ps (standard deviation). We present an enhanced White Rabbit (WR)-based clock synchronization system designed to meet this requirement. A noise-budget analysis of the standard WR slave loop identifies the analog actuation chain (DAC + VCXO + multiplier PLL) and restart-induced timing uncertainty as the dominant limitations. In our redesigned node, the DAC+VCXO chain is replaced by a Si5345A DSPLL clock generator with DCO-based phase control, removing the board-level analog tuning stage. GTX transceiver phase alignment and manual byte-alignment fixing reduce restart uncertainty from 88.8~ps to 12~ps peak-to-peak. For multi-node operation, we introduce a cascaded global-control architecture with PC-side PID auto-tuned by TD3 reinforcement learning, on-chip-temperature feed-forward calibrated to $-0.76\,\mathrm{ps}/^\circ\mathrm{C}$. The measured point-to-point synchronization precision is 3.38~ps over 1~m fiber and 3.92~ps over 50~km. In a 12-level cascade, the end-node precision reaches 6.66~ps at constant temperature and 7.30~ps under a 13$\,^\circ$C temperature swing. Synchronized-clock TIE jitter stays below 1~ps regardless of cascade depth. Restart uncertainty is 2.82~ps (std.\ dev.). A 4-level cascade operated stably for 25 hours of continuous monitoring. All measured metrics fall well within the CEPC 30~ps budget.

TL;DR: This paper reports the most precise measurement to date of the branching fraction B(D_s^+ → τ^+ ν_τ) and the product f_D_s^+ |V_cs| using 7.33 fb⁻¹ of e⁺e⁻ data at √s = 4.128–4.226 GeV collected by BESIII, analyzing four τ⁺ decay channels; it confirms lepton flavor universality via the ratio B(D_s^+ → τ^+ ν_τ)/B(D_s^+ → μ^+ ν_μ) = 10.04 ± 0.29.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: To test lepton flavor universality in charged-current weak decays and constrain the CKM matrix element |V_cs| and the D_s^+ decay constant f_D_s^+, both critical for Standard Model validation and new physics searches.

Method: Analysis of 7.33 fb⁻¹ of e⁺e⁻ collision data at center-of-mass energies 4.128–4.226 GeV recorded by the BESIII detector; extraction of B(D_s^+ → τ^+ ν_τ) via four τ⁺ decay modes (e⁺ν_eν̄_τ, μ⁺ν_μν̄_τ, π⁺ν̄_τ, π⁺π⁰ν̄_τ); background suppression using kinematic constraints and particle identification; simultaneous fit to signal yields across decay channels; determination of f_D_s^+ |V_cs| using theoretical relation between B(D_s^+ → ℓ⁺ ν_ℓ) and f_D_s^+ |V_cs|².

Result: B(D_s^+ → τ^+ ν_τ) = (5.37 ± 0.08_stat ± 0.06_syst) %; f_D_s^+ |V_cs| = (248.2 ± 1.9_stat ± 1.4_syst ± 0.6_input ± 0.8_EM) MeV; |V_cs| = 0.993 ± 0.008_stat ± 0.006_syst ± 0.003_input ± 0.003_EM (using lattice f_D_s^+); f_D_s^+ = (255.0 ± 1.9_stat ± 1.4_syst ± 0.6_input ± 0.8_EM) MeV (using CKMfitter |V_cs|); B(D_s^+ → τ^+ ν_τ)/B(D_s^+ → μ^+ ν_μ) = 10.04 ± 0.29, consistent with SM.

Conclusion: The results provide the world's most precise determinations of B(D_s^+ → τ^+ ν_τ) and f_D_s^+ |V_cs|, strongly supporting lepton flavor universality in the SM and placing stringent constraints on possible new physics contributions to charged-current D_s decays.

Abstract: Using a dataset of 7.33 fb$^{-1}$ of $e^+ e^-$ annihilation data collected with the BESIII detector at center-of-mass energies from 4.128 to 4.226 GeV, we report an updated measurement of the branching fraction of $D_s^+ \to τ^+ ν_τ$ via four $τ^+$ decay modes: $τ^+ \to e^+ ν_e \barν_τ$, $μ^+ ν_μ \barν_τ$, $π^+\barν_τ$, and $π^+ π^{0} \barν_τ$. The branching fraction is determined to be $\mathcal{B}({D_s^+ \to τ^+ ν_τ}) = (5.37 \pm 0.08_{\rm stat} \pm 0.06_{\rm syst})$ %. The product of the modulus of the Cabibbo-Kobayashi-Maskawa matrix element $|V_{cs}|$ and the $D_s^+$ decay constant $f_{D_s^+}$ is measured to be $f_{D_s^+} |V_{cs}| = (248.2 \pm 1.9_{\rm stat} \pm 1.4_{\rm syst} \pm 0.6_{\rm input} \pm 0.8_{\rm EM})$ MeV. Both the branching fraction and the product $f_{D_s^+} |V_{cs}|$ are the most precise results yet obtained. Then, taking $f_{D_s^+}$ from lattice quantum chromodynamics calculations results in $|V_{cs}| = 0.993 \pm 0.008_{\rm stat} \pm 0.006_{\rm syst} \pm 0.003_{\rm input} \pm 0.003_{\rm EM}$. Conversely, one finds $f_{D_s^+} = (255.0 \pm 1.9_{\rm stat} \pm 1.4_{\rm syst} \pm 0.6_{\rm input} \pm 0.8_{\rm EM})$ MeV when taking $|V_{cs}|$ from the CKMfitter group as an input. Combining with the world average value of $D_s^+ \to μ^+ ν_μ$, the ratio of the branching fractions between $D_s^+ \to τ^+ ν_τ$ and $D_s^+ \to μ^+ ν_μ$ is estimated to be $10.04 \pm 0.29$, which is consistent with the Standard Model prediction of lepton flavor universality.
