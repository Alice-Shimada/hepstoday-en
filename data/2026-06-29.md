The general formula was discovered by Claude Opus 4.6 working under our guidance, beginning with our earlier discovery of a one-term expression valid in the "simplest" kinematic chamber. Our results resolve the puzzle raised by Y.V. Lvov's 1997 computation of the five-wave amplitudes, unifying and extending it to all multiplicities.
