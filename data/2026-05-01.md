*CMS Collaboration*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: This paper presents improved measurements of Higgs boson pair (HH) production in the 4b final state using CMS data at 13 TeV and 13.6 TeV, achieving the most stringent limits to date on the HH signal strength μ_HH and Higgs self-couplings via combined resolved/merged jet topologies and luminosity-weighted combination.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: To probe the Higgs boson trilinear self-coupling (λ) and quartic HVVH coupling—key predictions of the Standard Model—via non-resonant HH production, which is highly suppressed and thus requires enhanced sensitivity in the dominant but challenging 4b decay channel.

Method: Analysis of pp collision data from CMS at √s = 13 TeV (138 fb⁻¹) and 13.6 TeV (62 fb⁻¹); exclusive treatment of HH → 4b events reconstructed in resolved (two small-R jets per H) and merged (one large-R jet per H) topologies; advanced trigger and event selection; profile likelihood-based signal extraction; statistical combination across topologies and energies.

Result: Combined 13+13.6 TeV result yields observed (expected) 95% CL upper limit μ_HH < 4.7 (2.8); resolved-only analysis with 138 fb⁻¹ gives μ_HH < 10.0 (5.9); 13.6 TeV alone gives μ_HH < 4.4 (4.4); improvements of >2× (resolved) and modest (merged) over prior 4b results; strongest constraints to date on λ and HVVH quartic coupling.

Conclusion: The analyses significantly advance sensitivity to Higgs self-interactions in the 4b channel, narrowing allowed parameter space for BSM physics affecting Higgs potential and electroweak symmetry breaking; methodology sets new benchmark for future HH searches with higher luminosity.

Abstract: Measurements of Higgs boson pair (HH) production in the four bottom quark (4b) final state are presented using proton-proton (pp) collision data at $\sqrt{s}$ = 13.6 TeV collected by the CMS experiment at the CERN LHC, corresponding to an integrated luminosity of 62 fb$^{-1}$. Events in which the Higgs boson decays, H$\mathrm{t\bar{t}}$, are separately reconstructed as pairs of small-radius jets (resolved), as well as those where they are reconstructed as single large-radius jets (merged), are studied exclusively. Compared to previously published LHC results in the 4b final state, the expected limit with an equivalent integrated luminosity is improved by more than a factor of two in the resolved topology and is better in the merged topology as well. An updated analysis of the resolved topology using 138 fb$^{-1}$ of 13 TeV pp collision data yields an observed (expected) 95% CL upper limit on $μ_\mathrm{HH}$ of 10.0 (5.9), an improvement of about 25% in the expected limit compared to the published results using the same data. Results in the 4b final state with 13 and 13.6 TeV are combined, resulting in an observed (expected) 95% CL upper limit on $μ_\mathrm{HH}$ of 4.7 (2.8). The allowed ranges for the Higgs boson trilinear self-coupling and quartic coupling between two Higgs bosons and two vector bosons are also reported. These are the most stringent constraints achieved in the 4b final state to date.
