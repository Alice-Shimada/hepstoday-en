### [2] [A Covariant Phase Space Approach to Einstein-AEther Gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28851)
*Walter Arata,Stefano Liberati,Giulio Neri*

Main category: gr-qc

TL;DR: This paper derives a consistent first law of black hole thermodynamics in Einstein-AEther gravity—a Lorentz-violating theory—by introducing disformal frames where causal horizons become Killing horizons, enabling a Wald-type derivation; it identifies an irreducible entropic AEther contribution to entropy and heat, extends thermodynamics to varying couplings (yielding generalized Smarr relations), and resolves a long-standing tension in universal-horizon thermodynamics by properly accounting for the AEther’s independent thermodynamic role.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: To resolve subtleties in black hole thermodynamics arising from multiple propagation speeds and distinct causal horizons in Lorentz-violating gravity—specifically Einstein-AEther theory—where standard Wald formalism fails without modification.

Method: Uses covariant phase space formalism with boundaries; introduces a disformal frame tailored to a given mode speed $c_s$ so its causal horizon becomes a Killing horizon, enabling standard Wald derivation; maps result back to original frame; extends framework to allow variation of theory couplings for extended thermodynamics; analyzes probe-mode limit $c_s \to +\infty$ to connect with universal-horizon thermodynamics.

Result: Derives a first law with horizon charge containing both Komar (gravitational) and irreducible AEther contributions; shows total entropy splits into gravitational and AEther parts, with the latter interpretable as heat from AEther flux; obtains generalized Smarr relations in extended thermodynamics; reconciles two conflicting universal-horizon temperature/entropy prescriptions by correctly isolating the AEther’s thermodynamic role.

Conclusion: A consistent, frame-invariant black hole first law exists in Einstein-AEther gravity when accounting for mode-specific causal structure via disformal frames; the AEther carries independent thermodynamic degrees of freedom—its flux contributes heat and entropy—and this resolves prior ambiguities in universal-horizon thermodynamics.

Abstract: Black hole thermodynamics in Lorentz-violating gravity is subtle because different excitations propagate at different speeds and hence identify different causal horizons. We revisit Einstein--AEther gravity using the covariant phase space formalism with boundaries and derive a consistent first law for stationary black holes. For a mode of propagation speed $c_s$, we introduce a disformal frame in which the corresponding causal horizon is a Killing horizon, so that the standard Wald-type derivation can be carried out. The result is then mapped back to the original frame, where it mantains the same structure. The associated horizon charge contains, besides the usual Komar term, an irreducible entropic AEther contribution that can be interpreted as heat due to the AEther flux across the horizon; accordingly, the total entropy splits into a gravitational part and an AEther part. We further develop an extended-thermodynamics framework in which the couplings of the theory are allowed to vary, obtaining generalized Smarr relations. Finally, we analyze the probe-mode limit $c_s \to +\infty$, clarifying its connection to universal-horizon thermodynamics and resolving the apparent tension in the literature between approaches that (i) fix the entropy to be proportional to the area and infer a corresponding temperature, and (ii) impose the Hawking temperature associated with modes peeling from the universal horizon and infer the entropy. Once the independent AEther contribution is properly taken into account, the two prescriptions are reconciled.
