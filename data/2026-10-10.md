### [1] [A massive right-handed sterile neutrino in three-body ${\bar B}\to D^*\ell^-{\bar N}_R$ decays - a Monte Carlo simulation study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00488)
*Bhubanjyoti Bhattacharya,Thomas E. Browder,Lucien M. Cremaldi,Horacio Crotte Ledesma,Nilakshi Das,Alakabha Datta,Tejhas Kapoor,Kumar Pandey,Alexei Sibidanov*

Main category: hep-ph

TL;DR: This paper develops a new Monte Carlo event generator within the EvtGen framework to simulate the B-bar → D* ℓ⁻ N̄_R decay, where N_R is a heavy right-handed neutrino; it validates the generator against analytical calculations of kinematic and angular observables (e.g., q², missing mass, A_FB, S₃, S₅), and assesses Belle II’s sensitivity to RHNs via these observables.

Motivation: To enable experimental searches for heavy right-handed neutrinos (RHNs) in semileptonic B decays by providing a validated, flexible Monte Carlo tool capable of simulating beyond-Standard-Model signatures.

Method: Development and validation of a custom Monte Carlo event generator based on EvtGen for the process \bar{B}→D^*\ell^-\bar{N}_R; comparison of simulated distributions (q², missing mass, forward-backward asymmetry A_FB, angular coefficients S₃, S₅) with analytical predictions.

Result: The generator is validated across key kinematic and angular observables; sensitivity studies identify optimal observables for RHN detection and confirm feasibility of such searches at Belle II.

Conclusion: The new generator provides a reliable simulation framework for RHN searches in \bar{B}→D^*\ell^-\bar{N}_R decays, supporting upcoming analyses at Belle II and enhancing discovery potential for heavy sterile neutrinos.

Abstract: We investigate the differential decay distributions of $\bar{B}\to D^*\ell^-{\bar N}_R $, where $N_R$ is a heavy right-handed neutrino (RHN) and $\ell = e, μ$. We employ a newly developed Monte Carlo event generator based on the EvtGen framework that can simulate beyond-the-Standard-Model processes. We validate the implementation of the model by comparing the Monte-Carlo results with analytical results for several kinematic variables such as $q^2$ and missing mass, as well as signatures in the angular distributions such as $A_{FB}$, $S_{3}$, $S_{5}$, etc. This MC Generator will enable new experimental searches for RHNs. We discuss the sensitivity of various observables to the presence of an RHN and the feasibility of an experimental analysis at Belle II.
