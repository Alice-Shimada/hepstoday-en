### [1] [A massive right-handed sterile neutrino in three-body ${\bar B}\to D^*\ell^-{\bar N}_R$ decays - a Monte Carlo simulation study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00488)
*Bhubanjyoti Bhattacharya,Thomas E. Browder,Lucien M. Cremaldi,Horacio Crotte Ledesma,Nilakshi Das,Alakabha Datta,Tejhas Kapoor,Kumar Pandey,Alexei Sibidanov*

Main category: hep-ph

TL;DR: This paper develops and validates a new Monte Carlo event generator (based on EvtGen) to simulate the B-bar → D* ℓ⁻ N̄_R decay—a probe of heavy right-handed neutrinos—and assesses its experimental sensitivity at Belle II.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: To enable robust experimental searches for heavy right-handed neutrinos (RHNs) via rare semileptonic B decays, which are sensitive probes of lepton number violation and neutrino mass generation mechanisms beyond the Standard Model.

Method: Implementation of a custom Monte Carlo event generator within the EvtGen framework for the process \bar{B}→D^*\ell^-\bar{N}_R; validation against analytical calculations for kinematic (q², missing mass) and angular observables (A_FB, S₃, S₅); sensitivity studies using realistic experimental considerations for Belle II.

Result: Successful validation of the MC generator against analytical predictions across key differential distributions; quantified sensitivity of angular and kinematic observables to RHN mass and mixing parameters; demonstration of feasibility for RHN searches at Belle II with projected constraints.

Conclusion: The validated generator provides a reliable tool for designing and interpreting RHN searches in \bar{B}→D^*\ell^-\bar{N}_R at Belle II and future B-factories, enhancing discovery potential for sterile neutrinos in the GeV–few-GeV mass range.

Abstract: We investigate the differential decay distributions of $\bar{B}\to D^*\ell^-{\bar N}_R $, where $N_R$ is a heavy right-handed neutrino (RHN) and $\ell = e, μ$. We employ a newly developed Monte Carlo event generator based on the EvtGen framework that can simulate beyond-the-Standard-Model processes. We validate the implementation of the model by comparing the Monte-Carlo results with analytical results for several kinematic variables such as $q^2$ and missing mass, as well as signatures in the angular distributions such as $A_{FB}$, $S_{3}$, $S_{5}$, etc. This MC Generator will enable new experimental searches for RHNs. We discuss the sensitivity of various observables to the presence of an RHN and the feasibility of an experimental analysis at Belle II.
