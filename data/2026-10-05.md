Abstract: We quantize the exterior region of an odd-dimensional Kerr-AdS black hole, in which all rotational parameters are equal, using our model of quantum gravity. Because the temporal Hamiltonian is the same in both regions, the interior and exterior eigenvalues coincide, and this choice defines a unitary equivalence between the respective Hilbert spaces and Hamiltonians. The unitary equivalence between the Hilbert spaces and their respective Hamiltonians preserves the complete quantum-statistical information carried by all normal states in the interior and exterior regions of the black hole. Hence, there is no information paradox at the quantum level.
