It argues that these excesses, though currently marginal, occur in low-background regions and are promising for discovery in LHC Run 3; further, it links them to anomalous triple Higgs coupling (κλ) measurements and highlights complementary searches in ZZ/WW final states and precision electroweak constraints.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: Interpretation of existing LHC resonance search results—including previously reported KK graviton candidates at 380 GeV and 700 GeV, and new ATLAS excesses in the 1000–1300 GeV region—in the context of the RS model; analysis of local cross section deviations from SM expectations for varying κλ; assessment of discovery potential in low-background channels (e.g., triple Higgs, ZZ/WW semi-leptonic/hadronic); incorporation of indirect constraints from electroweak precision observables.
