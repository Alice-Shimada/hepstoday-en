Motivation: To identify and characterize the exotic resonance X(2370) and test its compatibility with the long-predicted pseudoscalar glueball, leveraging high-statistics J/ψ radiative decays as a clean glue-rich environment.

Method: Analysis of (10087±44)×10⁶ J/ψ events from BESIII; invariant mass spectroscopy of K⁰_SK⁰_Sπ⁰ and π⁰π⁰η final states; amplitude analysis to extract resonance parameters and branching fractions; statistical significance estimation via likelihood fits and background modeling.

Result: X(2370) observed in K⁰_SK⁰_Sπ⁰ (14σ) and π⁰π⁰η (20σ); combined mass = 2359⁺¹³₋₁₄ MeV/c², width = 170⁺⁴⁴₋₂₉ MeV; first observation of X(2370) → a₀(980)⁰π⁰ (≥9σ); decay pattern mirrors η_c, supporting pseudoscalar glueball interpretation.

Conclusion: X(2370) is strongly favored as the lightest pseudoscalar glueball candidate, based on its mass, width, production in glue-favored J/ψ radiative decays, and characteristic decays involving scalar mesons (a₀(980)) and pseudoscalar pairs.

Abstract: Based on $(10087\pm44)\times10^{6}$ $J/ψ$ events collected with the BESIII detector, the $J/ψ\rightarrowγK^{0}_{S}K^{0}_{S}π^{0}$ and $J/ψ\rightarrowγπ^{0}π^{0}η$ processes are studied. Combining measurements from these processes with measurements from the previously reported $J/ψ\rightarrowγK^{0}_{S}K^{0}_{S}η^{\prime}$ process, the mass and width of the $X(2370)$ are determined to be $2359^{+13}_{-14}~\text{MeV}/c^{2}$ and $170^{+44}_{-29}~\text{MeV}$, respectively. In addition, the decay $X(2370)\to a_{0}(980)^{0}π^{0}$ with $a_{0}(980)^{0}\to π^{0}η$ is observed with a statistical significance exceeding $9σ$. The similarities in decay modes between the $X(2370)$ and $η_{c}$ are consistent with the features of a pseudoscalar glueball.
