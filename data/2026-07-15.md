### [2] [Observation of pair production of longitudinally polarized $Z$ bosons in four-lepton final state with the ATLAS detector](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12879)
*ATLAS Collaboration*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: This paper reports the first observation of two simultaneously longitudinally polarized Z bosons produced in pp collisions at 13.6 TeV, using ATLAS data (164 fb⁻¹); polarization fractions are measured inclusively and in mass bins, agreeing with SM predictions, and combined with prior 13 TeV results yield 6.5σ (obs.) / 5.6σ (exp.) significance.


<details>
