Method: Covariant baryon chiral perturbation theory with extended-on-mass-shell (EOMS) renormalization; includes systematic inclusion of relativistic corrections, explicit spin-3/2 decuplet baryons, two spin-3/2 coupling schemes (consistent vs. standard), and pion-loop contributions; uses same diagrammatic topologies as early heavy-baryon analyses for controlled comparison.

Result: Relativistic corrections induce only mild changes to |ΔI|=3/2 amplitudes; decuplet contributions—especially in the consistent-coupling scheme—substantially improve χ²; pion-loop contributions further reduce χ²; consistent coupling is identified as essential for accurate description of the amplitudes.

Conclusion: The consistent-coupling scheme for spin-3/2 baryons, combined with pion loops, is crucial for describing |ΔI|=3/2 amplitudes in non-leptonic hyperon decays; this underscores the need for proper treatment of decuplet dynamics in relativistic chiral EFT analyses of weak baryon decays.

Abstract: Inspired by the recent BESIII measurements of non-leptonic hyperon decays, we reexamine their $|ΔI|=3/2$ amplitudes in covariant baryon chiral perturbation theory with the extended-on-mass-shell renormalization scheme. Using the same restricted set of diagrammatic topologies as in the early analyses in heavy baryon chiral perturbation theory, we assess the effects of relativistic corrections, explicit decuplet baryons, different spin-$3/2$ coupling schemes, and pion-loop contributions. Our results show that relativistic effects alone lead to only mild changes, while decuplet contributions, especially in the consistent-coupling scheme, significantly improve the fit quality. Pion-loop contributions further reduce the $χ^2$ values. We highlight the importance of the consistent coupling scheme in the decuplet sector for describing the selected $|ΔI|=3/2$ amplitudes.
