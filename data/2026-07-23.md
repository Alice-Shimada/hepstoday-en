### [48] [Nucleon unpolarized second Mellin moments using lattice QCD ensembles with physical quark masses and in the continuum limit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20337)
*Constantia Alexandrou,Simone Bacchio,Jacob Finkenrath,Christos Iona,Giannis Koutsou,Christian Kummer,Yan Li,Bhavna Prasad,Gregoris Spanoudes*

Main category: hep-lat

TL;DR: This paper computes nucleon energy-momentum tensor matrix elements using physical-value twisted-mass clover-improved fermion ensembles at four lattice spacings to perform a direct continuum extrapolation at the physical pion mass; it includes non-perturbative renormalization, separates connected/disconnected quark and gluon contributions, and extracts gravitational form factors at Q²=0 to quantify quark/gluon contributions to proton momentum, spin, and orbital angular momentum.

Motivation: To determine the internal mechanical structure of the nucleon—specifically how quarks and gluons contribute to its momentum, spin, and angular momentum—by computing the energy-momentum tensor matrix elements with controlled systematics, including continuum extrapolation at the physical point and non-perturbative renormalization.

Method: Lattice QCD simulations using four twisted-mass clover-improved fermion ensembles with near-physical u/d/s/c quark masses, fixed physical volume, and lattice spacings a = 0.049–0.080 fm; computation of connected/disconnected quark and gluon contributions to the energy-momentum tensor; non-perturbative determination of renormalization constants, including quark-singlet–gluon mixing; extraction of gravitational form factors (A20(0), J) at Q² = 0 via continuum limit.

Result: Gravitational form factors A20(0) and J extracted in the continuum limit at Q² = 0; decomposition of proton momentum and total angular momentum into quark (up, down, strange) and gluon contributions; flavor-separated orbital angular momentum derived by combining EMT results with previously computed intrinsic quark spin values.

Conclusion: Quarks carry ~50% of the proton’s momentum and ~30% of its total angular momentum at the scale studied; gluons contribute significantly to both momentum and angular momentum; orbital angular momentum is substantial and flavor-dependent, underscoring the need for full QCD treatment beyond valence-quark pictures.

Abstract: We compute the matrix elements of the energy-momentum tensor of the nucleon using four ensembles of twisted mass clover-improved fermions with the up, down, strange and charm quark masses tuned to approximately their physical values. The four ensembles have similar physical volume and lattice spacings $a=0.080$~fm, $0.068$~fm, $0.057$~fm, and $0.049$ fm, allowing us to take the continuum limit directly at the physical pion mass point. We compute both connected and disconnected quark contributions as well as gluon contributions. All renormalization functions, including the mixing of the quark singlet with the gluon, are determined non-perturbatively. We extract the gravitational form factors in the continuum limit at $Q^2=0$ and evaluate the contribution of quarks and gluons to the momentum and angular momentum of the proton. Using the values of the intrinsic quark spin computed using the same gauge ensembles we also determine the orbital angular momentum for each quark flavor.
