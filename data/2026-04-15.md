TL;DR: This paper reports a high-precision measurement of the W-boson production cross-section in the W → μν channel using 5.1 fb⁻¹ of LHCb pp collision data at √s = 13 TeV, yielding σ(W⁺→μ⁺ν) = 1754.2 ± 1.5 ± 11.9 ± 35.1 pb and σ(W⁻→μ⁻ν̄) = 1178.1 ± 1.3 ± 9.7 ± 23.6 pb, consistent with NNLO QCD predictions and surpassing prior precision in the forward muon kinematic region (2.0 < η < 4.5, 25 < p_T < 55 GeV).

Motivation: To improve the precision of W-boson production cross-section measurements in the forward region—where LHCb has unique acceptance—and to provide a stringent test of NNLO QCD predictions, complementing ATLAS/CMS central-region measurements.

Method: Analysis of proton-proton collision data at √s = 13 TeV collected by LHCb; selection of W → μν events with muons in 25–55 GeV p_T and 2.0–4.5 pseudorapidity; background estimation via data-driven techniques; correction for detector effects using simulation; luminosity determination from van der Meer scans; full uncertainty breakdown (statistical, systematic, luminosity).

Result: σ(W⁺→μ⁺ν) = 1754.2 ± 1.5 (stat) ± 11.9 (syst) ± 35.1 (lumi) pb; σ(W⁻→μ⁻ν̄) = 1178.1 ± 1.3 (stat) ± 9.7 (syst) ± 23.6 (lumi) pb; both consistent with NNLO QCD theory within uncertainties; ~2–3× improved precision over previous LHCb and comparable forward-region measurements.

Conclusion: The measurement establishes a new benchmark for W production in the forward region, validates NNLO QCD calculations with unprecedented precision in this kinematic regime, and demonstrates LHCb’s capability for high-precision electroweak physics beyond flavor studies.

Abstract: A precision measurement of the $W$-boson production cross-section is performed using the $W \to μν$ decay channel, based on a sample of proton-proton collision data collected by the LHCb experiment at $\sqrt{s}$ = 13 TeV and corresponding to an integrated luminosity of 5.1 $fb^{-1}$. The cross-section is measured for muons with transverse momentum between 25 and 55 GeV and pseudorapidity between 2.0 and 4.5. The integrated production cross-sections of $W$ bosons are measured to be $$ \begin{array}{lcl} σ_{W^+ \to μ^+ν} &=& 1754.2 \pm 1.5 \pm 11.9 \pm 35.1\text{ pb} \\ σ_{W^- \to μ^-\barν} &=& 1178.1 \pm 1.3 \pm 9.7 \pm 23.6\text{ pb} \end{array} $$ where uncertainties are statistical, systematic, and due to the luminosity determination, respectively. Results are in good agreement with theoretical predictions at next-to-next-to-leading order in perturbative quantum chromodynamics. This measurement is significantly more precise than previous results in this kinematic regime.
