### [1] [Optimal binning of $D\rightarrow K_{\mathrm S}^0π^+π^-$ and $D\rightarrow K_{\mathrm S}^0K^+K^-$ phase space for experimental measurements](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13948)
*Marcelo Bovill,Nathan Jurik,Sneha Malde*

Main category: hep-ex

TL;DR: Proposes new optimized binning schemes for D→K⁰ₛπ⁺π⁻ and D→K⁰ₛK⁺K⁻ Dalitz plots to improve γ and charm mixing parameter (x_CP, Δx) measurements; achieves ~5% γ precision gain and ~20% statistical sensitivity gain for x_CP/Δx while controlling bin-migration bias.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Improve precision of CKM angle γ and charm mixing parameters (x_CP, Δx) by optimizing Dalitz plot binning with a physics-motivated figure of merit that accounts for γ sensitivity, degeneracies with other fitted parameters, and realistic background modeling.

Method: Introduces a new figure of merit for Dalitz plot binning optimization tailored to γ sensitivity; incorporates simultaneous fit degeneracies and refined background treatment; applies resolution-aware optimization including event migration effects for x_CP and Δx observables in D→K⁰ₛπ⁺π⁻.

Result: Estimated ~5% improvement in γ precision from B±→DK± with D→K⁰ₛπ⁺π⁻; first dedicated optimization for x_CP and Δx yields ~20% gain in statistical sensitivity while keeping bin-migration-induced bias comparable to current scheme.

Conclusion: The proposed binning schemes significantly enhance sensitivity to γ and charm mixing parameters by employing a more physically relevant optimization metric and realistic experimental effects—setting a new standard for Dalitz analysis in charm and B physics.

Abstract: New binning schemes for the Dalitz plots of $D \rightarrow K^0_{\mathrm S}π^+π^-$ and $D \rightarrow K^0_{\mathrm S} K^+ K^-$ decays are presented. These are determined using a new figure of merit for optimisation that better represents the sensitivity to $γ$ than previous metrics. Further augmentation comes from including consideration of degeneracy with other physics parameters determined simultaneously and a more accurate treatment of relevant backgrounds. The overall expected improvement in the precision of $γ$ measurements using $B^\pm\to DK^\pm$ decays, followed by $D \rightarrow K^0_{\mathrm S}π^+π^-$, is estimated at around 5$\%$. In addition, the first dedicated optimisation is performed for the observables relevant to charm mixing, $x_{CP}$ and $Δx$, in $D \rightarrow K^0_{\mathrm S}π^+π^-$ decays. This procedure accounts for both statistical sensitivity and the migration of events between bins due to detector resolution. The resulting binning scheme leads to an estimated gain of approximately 20$\%$ in statistical sensitivity, while maintaining the bias due to bin migration at a level comparable to the currently used scheme.
