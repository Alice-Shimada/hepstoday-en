*H. Arthur Weldon*

Main category: gr-qc

TL;DR: This paper derives a general superpotential formula for gravitational Lagrangians depending arbitrarily on the Riemann tensor and its derivatives, using Noether’s second theorem; it applies to diffeomorphism-invariant theories including f(R) and quadratic gravity, and extends to matter sectors with metric-derivative couplings.


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: To systematically construct conserved currents and superpotentials for generally covariant gravitational theories with arbitrary dependence on the Riemann tensor and its derivatives — beyond standard Einstein–Hilbert or Lovelock forms.

Method: Applies Noether's second theorem separately to the gravitational Lagrangian $\mathcal{L}_G$ (arbitrary Riemann/differential dependence) and matter Lagrangian $\mathcal{L}_M$ (scalar field with possible metric-derivative couplings); exploits diffeomorphism invariance to derive identities for the generalized Einstein tensor and a Noether current $J^\alpha_{G2}$, then solves $\partial_\mu \Phi^{[\alpha\mu]} = \sqrt{g}\, J^\alpha_{G2}$ for the superpotential $\Phi^{[\alpha\mu]}$.

Result: An explicit, closed-form expression for the superpotential $\Phi^{[\alpha\mu]}$ satisfying $\partial_\mu \Phi^{[\alpha\mu]} = \sqrt{g}\, J^\alpha_{G2}$; verification and application to $f(R)$ gravity and quadratic gravity (e.g., Gauss–Bonnet, $R_{\mu\nu}R^{\mu\nu}$); analogous superpotential construction for matter sectors with derivative couplings to the metric.

Conclusion: The derived superpotential provides a universal, geometrically consistent tool for defining conserved charges in higher-derivative, diffeomorphism-invariant gravity theories; it unifies and generalizes prior results for specific models and extends conservation laws to derivative-coupled matter.

Abstract: This paper investigates Lagrangians with arbitrary dependence on the Riemann tensor and any number of derivatives. Noether's second theorem is applied separately to ${\cal L}_{G}$ and ${\cal L}_{M}$, where the matter Lagrangian depends on a scalar field. For ${\cal L}_{G}$ diffeomorphism invariance requires that the generalized Einstein tensor satisfy $\nabla_αE^{αβ}=0$ and that there is a current satisfying $0=\partial_α(\sqrt{g}\, J^α_{G2})$. The main result is an explicit formula for the superpotential $Φ^{[αμ]}$ that solves $\partial_μΦ^{[αμ]}=\sqrt{g}\,J^α_{G2}$. Some applications to $f(R)$ gravity and various quadratic gravity theories are discussed. There are analogous results, including superpotentials, for the matter sector whenever the couplings to the matter fields contain derivatives of the metric.
