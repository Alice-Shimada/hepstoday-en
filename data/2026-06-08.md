In order to get Shannon entropy, a novel factorization method is introduced. This is facilitated through the use of well-known weighted Lebesgue measure. The results from the Dunkl case agree exactly with the non-Dunkl scenario, when Dunkl parameters vanish. The reflection operators and Dunkl parameters considerably influence the above measures. These are portrayed in graphical forms.
